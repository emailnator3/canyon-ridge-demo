# Spanish Texas Electrician Exam Prep — Launch Kit

Goal: sell the first Spanish-language practice product built on the NEC 2026 for the
Texas Journeyman exam. Texas switched exams to the 2026 NEC on Sept 1, 2026, and TDLR
offers every electrician exam in Spanish.

## Product v1 (sell this first, nothing bigger)
**"Simulacro de Cálculos — Journeyman Texas, NEC 2026"**
- 50 calculation questions, Spanish first with English terms in parentheses
- Worked solution for every question, citing the NEC section number only (never copy NEC text; NFPA owns it)
- Delivered as: PDF + self-grading Google Forms quiz link
- Price: $24 (under Ray Holder's English Texas guide at ~$36)
- Why calculations first: FY2025 Journeyman Calculations pass rate was 20.56% (5,027 fails), and
  candidates retake only the part they failed.

## The weekly system (target: 7–9 hrs/week of your time)
| Step | Who does it | Your time/week |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Draft 10–15 questions per batch by NEC article, with worked answers | AI (Claude/ChatGPT) | ~30 min prompting |
| 2. Check every answer and section number against your NEC 2026 book | **You** | 3–4 hrs |
| 3. Translate to Spanish trade terms | AI | ~15 min |
| 4. Check the Spanish reads like a Texas jobsite, not a dictionary | **Bilingual reviewer** (paid per batch) | 0 (you send/receive) |
| 5. Paste into Google Forms quiz + Google Doc → PDF | You (copy/paste) | ~1 hr |
| 6. Post in Spanish-language trade groups, answer messages | **You** | 2–3 hrs |
| Delivery, grading, payment | Payhip/Gumroad + Google Forms (automatic) | 0 |

## Milestones and kill signals
- **Week 1:** records request sent; reviewer post up; 10 sample questions checked.
- **Week 2–3:** reviewer found; sample quiz posted free in 5+ groups; collect emails/WhatsApp interest.
- **Week 4–5:** 50-question product live at $24.
- **KILL if:** fewer than 10 paid sales within 45 days of posting in at least 5 groups,
  or TDLR's records show only a tiny share of exams taken in Spanish.
- **SCALE if it works:** Code-knowledge (NEC) drill → Master version → contractor crew bundle → California (also offers Spanish exams).

## Rules that protect you
1. Never paste NEC text into a product. Cite section numbers and explain in your own words.
2. Nothing ships until **you** have checked the code answer and **the reviewer** has checked the Spanish.
3. Re-check every section number against the 2026 book — some articles were renumbered between editions.
