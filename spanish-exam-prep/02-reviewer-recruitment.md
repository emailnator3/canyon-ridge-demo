# Finding a bilingual reviewer

**Who you need:** a licensed Texas journeyman or master who reads and writes Spanish well
and talks to Spanish-speaking crews every day. Not a translator — a tradesman.

**What they do:** read each batch of 10–15 questions and fix any Spanish that a Texas
electrician wouldn't say. You still own the code answers.

**Suggested pay (your call):** a flat fee per 50 questions reviewed, OR 15–20% of sales of
the product they reviewed. Offer both; let them choose. Do a paid trial on 10 questions first.

**Where to post / ask:**
1. 2–3 Texas electrician Facebook groups (English and Spanish ones)
2. Your existing customer list — some of your past buyers may be bilingual
3. IEC Fort Worth — they ran a Spanish-taught Journeyman prep course in early 2026;
   ask if the instructor would review part-time (https://iec.flashpoint.xyz/Lite/Event/Profile/977597001)
